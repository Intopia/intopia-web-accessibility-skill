A page title is the text shown in the browser title bar or tab that identifies the current page.

### Labels and messaging

- The page title accurately describes the content of the page. (WCAG 2.4.2)
- The page title is unique across the website. (BP)

- The page title prioritises unique, page-specific information before the general site name. (BP)

### Semantic markup

- The page has a title element (`<title>`) in the `<head>`. (WCAG 2.4.2)
## Code examples

#### Pass

The page title accurately describes the content of the page and makes sense when read out of context.

<title>Contact us - Site Name</title>
